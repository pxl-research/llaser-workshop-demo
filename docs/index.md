In these exercises, you attack LLASER yourself, switch on defenses to stop your own attacks, and then let promptfoo attack LLASER automatically. You play both sides: the attacker (red team) and the defender (blue team).

## Before you start: upload the documents

Download these three PDFs, and upload them with the upload button on the **Chat** page, if they are not uploaded yet:

- [01_course_info.pdf](corpus/01_course_info.pdf)
- [02_student_records.pdf](corpus/02_student_records.pdf)
- [03_confidential_memo.pdf](corpus/03_confidential_memo.pdf)

All three documents are fictional test data.

## Schedule

| Time | Part | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00 - 10:30 | Buffer and break | Setup done early? Start with [1a](part-1.html#1a-start-with-the-known-attacks-5-minutes). |
| 10:30 - 10:45 | [1. Attack by hand](part-1.html) | Try known attacks, then invent your own. |
| 10:45 - 11:15 | [2. Defend](part-2.html) | Switch on defenses one by one, then try to make a weak European model secure. |
| 11:15 - 11:45 | [3. Automated red teaming](part-3.html) | Measure with promptfoo whether the guardrails really make that model secure. |
| 11:45 - 12:00 | [Conclusions](conclusions.html) | Share what worked and what did not. |

Every step has a time box. When the time is up, move on to the next step, even if you are not finished. Steps marked *optional* are for when you have time left.
